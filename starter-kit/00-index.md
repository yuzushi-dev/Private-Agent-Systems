## Knowledge lifecycle

Agents prepare source packages in `source-staging/`. After review, an operator
promotes them to read-only `sources/`. Durable concepts live in `wiki/`.
Reviewed intake moves from `inbox/` to `processed/`. Questions are recorded in
`_queries/`.

## Operational memory

Project context, decisions, runbooks, daily notes, and archives remain outside
the wiki.
